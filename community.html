Community

We live here.

Our agents don’t just work in the Triangle’s neighborhoods. We live in them. We’re raising kids here, walking dogs here, and standing in line at the same coffee shops you do.

So when you see us at a brewery fundraiser, a pet adoption day, or a neighborhood festival, it isn’t a marketing plan. It’s just us, living our lives, same as you. Over ten years, that’s looked like many things: food deliveries, wild and domestic animal rescues, and volunteer home builds. Some of it is organized. Much of it is simply being present.

Being around is also how you learn about a place. You find out which streets flood after a storm and which porches have neighbors who wave. You learn where the new greenway is going and which pizza place is worth the drive. We pay attention to all of it and bring it with us every time we help someone find home here.

Along the way, our neighbors have honored us with awards we’re proud of.

Ten years. Ten gatherings. You’re invited.
