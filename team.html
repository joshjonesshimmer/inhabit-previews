Every Inhabit agent is a licensed North Carolina real estate broker who lives and works in the Triangle. Many came to real estate from other careers, like teaching, design, nonprofit work, social work, and technology, and they bring those skills with them. Each agent knows the Triangle’s neighborhoods like their own street. And each one has the whole firm to lean on: when your agent runs into something new, one of their colleagues has usually seen it before.

Inhabit is led by owner and Broker-In-Charge Tammi Brooks and Broker-In-Charge Connie Semans. They’ve built a firm where agents collaborate instead of compete, because that’s how clients get the best advice.

Not sure where to start? Tell us a little about your plans, and we’ll introduce you to someone who knows the way.
