How we work

Two paths, one person guiding you.

Buying

Typical timeline, 8 weeks

1
Coffee, no pitch
We learn what you actually want before we open a single door.
2
Get your number straight
Lender intros, budget reality check, monthly cost math.
3
Tour on your schedule
Nights and weekends included, because that is when you are free.
4
No stone unturned
Our agents leave no stone, or crawlspace, unturned before you make an offer.
