Selling

Every home has a story. We help buyers see it.

Selling a home is part money, part memory, and usually part logistics. We’ll help with all three.

Start with a conversationMeet the team

The 7 steps

  1. Tell us what’s next
  2. Find your home’s story
  3. Set your price with confidence
  4. Get your home ready
  5. Reach the right buyers
  6. Weigh your offers
  7. See it through to closing
  1. 1

    Step 1: Tell us what’s next

    Where are you headed, when do you need to move, and what do you need from this sale? Your answers shape everything that follows.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Find your home’s story

    Together, we’ll find the quality that will make the right buyer fall for your home. It might be a morning-light kitchen, a porch made for evenings, or a walk to the park. Then we build a marketing plan around that story and around you.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Set your price with confidence

    You’ll see the same recent sales and market activity we see. We’ll recommend the price we believe the market will support, not the number that sounds best at your kitchen table.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Get your home ready

    You’ll know which repairs are worth making and which to skip, with stagers, painters, and tradespeople we trust ready to help.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Reach the right buyers

    Your home gets professional photos, a description written by someone who has actually walked through it, and a listing that shows up where Triangle buyers are looking.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Weigh your offers

    The highest price isn’t always the best offer. We’ll compare due diligence fees, financing, timing, and terms so you can choose the offer most likely to close.

  7. 7

    Step 7: See it through to closing

    Your agent handles repair requests and appraisal questions and keeps things moving with the buyer’s agent and your closing attorney until the keys change hands.

Selling and buying at the same time?

With Inhabit, one agent can guide both your sale and your next purchase. That means one plan, one person keeping track of both timelines, and fewer moving parts to worry about.

We’ll talk through your options, like the order to do things in and whether a contingent offer or a short stay after closing makes sense.

Plan both moves with us

Whenever you’re ready to talk about what home means to you, we’re here.
