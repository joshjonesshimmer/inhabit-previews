Meet Stephanie

Growing up, my family moved fifteen times before I turned eighteen, so I know my way around a moving box! I used to design costumes for theatre and film; now I make outfits for our French Bulldog, Echo.

My husband, Matthew, and I were undefeated in mixed doubles, but for the good of our marriage, we now play just for fun. I love to throw a party and bring people together, and my phoenix dream is to be a game show host. Can you tell I’m an extrovert to the max?
