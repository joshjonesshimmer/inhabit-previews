Our Story

Let’s get you home!

We build a plan around your life, not around a sale. That’s been the idea since 2016, when we set out to build a real estate company that belonged to its neighbors: small enough to know the neighborhoods, committed enough to pick up the phone, and rooted deeply enough to still be around long after closing day.

All these years later, we still are. Inhabit is independent and woman-owned, with offices in Durham and Chapel Hill and agents who live all over the Triangle. Regional and national firms have asked more than once whether we’d sell. Our answer has been the same every time: Thanks. We’re good.

Thanks. We’re good.

Staying independent lets us serve clients the way we think they should be served. At a lot of brokerages, agents compete against each other. We work the other way, because clients are better off when agents share what they know. Our agents compare notes on everything, from new contract clauses to the lender who calls back on a Saturday. The market keeps changing, so we keep learning together. When you work with one Inhabit agent, you get the whole firm’s experience behind them.

That same thinking shapes the advice we give. Home is where your life happens, and it’s arguably the biggest financial decision you’ll make, so we listen first. We ask what you want your days to look like and what’s keeping you up at night. Then we tell you what we’d tell a friend, including “not this one” and “maybe not yet.”

Ten years in, we’re still local, still independent, and still the people who pick up the phone. Whenever you’re ready to talk about what home means to you, we’re here.
